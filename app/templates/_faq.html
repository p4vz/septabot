What can septabot do?

Ask the Telegram bot anything about your Philly commute. It pulls live data from SEPTA, NWS, 511PA, and Google Maps, then a small LLM writes a short answer with a recommendation.

It can

  • Report Regional Rail delays and stuck trains, line by line
  • Show next-to-arrive trains at any SEPTA station
  • Track live bus/trolley positions, next stop, and delay by route
  • Surface SEPTA service alerts and bus/trolley detours
  • Report out-of-service elevators/escalators (accessibility)
  • Give the Philly weather forecast and active NWS alerts
  • List 511PA traffic incidents (crashes, closures, construction) in the region
  • Driving directions with live traffic via Google Maps
  • SEPTA-aware door-to-door transit routes (walk + bus/train + walk, with fares)
  • Compare driving vs. transit for a given trip and recommend one

It can't

  • Buy tickets or reload your SEPTA Key
  • Draw vehicles on a live map (it reports positions as text, not a map view)
  • Predict future delays — it only reports the current state
  • Plan trips outside the Philadelphia region
  • Send proactive push notifications — it replies when you ask
  • Handle Amtrak, NJ Transit, or PATCO as first-class — Google may route through them, but SEPTA is the focus

Try asking

  • “How's the Paoli/Thorndale line right now?”
  • “Should I drive or take the train from Wayne to Center City?”
  • “Next train from Suburban Station to the airport?”
  • “Any crashes on the Schuylkill heading west?”
  • “Weather for my walk home in an hour?”
  • “Where's the 33 bus right now, and is it running late?”
  • “Is the elevator at 69th Street working?”
  • “Are there any bus detours near Temple right now?”
  • “Quickest way from 30th Street Station to Fishtown tonight?”
Data sources: SEPTA Open Data · NWS api.weather.gov · 511PA · Google Maps Platform. All data is fetched live and cached for a few minutes.
